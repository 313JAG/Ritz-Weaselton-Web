# Marriott Corporate & Promo Codes — The Complete List

> **362 codes** · Last updated: March 2026
> Discount ranges are approximate and vary by property, market, and dates. Always compare against Bonvoy member rates and prepay rates.

---

## Quick Reference

- **How to use:** Marriott.com → Special Rates → Corp/Promo Code → enter code → Find Hotels
- **Verification:** 20–50% chance of ID check at check-in. Higher at full-service city hotels, lower at suburban properties.
- **Perks:** Most corporate rates include flexible cancellation (often until noon on arrival), free wifi, and full Bonvoy points/elite credits.
- **Multiple codes:** Many companies have 2–3 codes. Always test alternates for the best rate.
- **BreakAway program (XML):** Allows corporate employees to use leisure weekend rates at up to 30% off. Expanded to 100+ companies.

## Key Notes

1. **Verification frequency** varies wildly. Full-service city hotels check more often. Suburban Courtyards almost never check. Anecdotal rate: 20–50%.

2. **Always compare rates.** Bonvoy Member Prepay Rate is non-refundable but often cheaper than any corporate code.

3. **BreakAway program (XML)** is a massive opportunity — it allows employees of 100+ major companies to use corporate-style discounts for personal weekend travel.

4. **Legacy codes** from defunct companies (Lehman, Wachovia, Washington Mutual) sometimes still work in Marriott's system.

5. **Australia coverage** is growing but limited. BHP, Suncorp, and Treasury Wine Estates are the most relevant local codes. Always test global chain-wide codes (DTC, ACC, GEE, GGL) at AU properties too.

6. **Multiple codes per company** — always test all alternates. The numeric codes sometimes yield different rates than the letter codes.
